Game Day Grub For The Smoker!

How to prep Chicken before smoking.

  1. Chicken Thigh's, Breast's, Or drumsticks.
  2. Some will cry about this step but boil chicken for 8min's
  3. remove from water
  4. Seasonings of your choice "mine are salt,pepper,garlic,onion powder, red pepper flakes.
  5. put in huge bowl and Toss in The Sauce "pick your favorite"!
  6. Put The chicken covered in sauce to the side and move to smoker prep.

Prep your smoker

  1. Load smoker with wood or pellets.
  2. Turn on or light fire to pre-heat.
  3. when hot scrap off grill and clean great.
  4. Add more Wood or pellets if needed.
  5. close lid pre-heat to 350F.

Cooking Instructions

  • Place chicken on clean grill
  • with remaining sauce in bowl Brush chicen
  • every 5-8Min flip chicken and brush with sauce
  • Cook Chicken till reaches 165F beofre removing
